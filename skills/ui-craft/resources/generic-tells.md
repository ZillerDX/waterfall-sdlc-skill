# Generic tells

These are defaults generated UI falls into regardless of subject. Each is legitimate when the brief asks for it — never as an unexamined default. When one appears in the plan or a screenshot, replace it with a choice specific to this brief.

## Palettes
- Cream/off-white ground + high-contrast serif + terracotta or clay accent. (Also closely resembles the Claude interface's own accent — reads as copied.)
- Near-black ground + one acid-green, neon-cyan, or vermilion accent.
- Purple→blue or pink→orange gradients as the brand.
- `#0B0B0B` / `#111` standing in for "black"; everything gray-on-gray.

## Layout
- Hero = big number + small label + a row of stats + gradient accent.
- Centered headline + subline + two buttons + 3 identical feature cards.
- SaaS card kit: every section chopped into identical rounded cards, same radius on everything regardless of hierarchy, same soft shadow under each, gradient washes behind.
- Newspaper look (hairline rules, zero radius, dense columns) applied to a non-editorial subject.
- Default pricing: 3 tiers, middle one "Most popular" with a glow.
- "Trusted by" strip of invented logos; testimonial carousel with invented names.

## Typography & chrome
- Tracked ALL-CAPS eyebrow label above every heading.
- One word in the headline colored, italicized, or gradient-filled.
- Gradient text on headings.
- Monospace for small labels and metadata "for tech feel".
- Meta strings joined with middle dots (A · B · C); labels as "WORD — fragment".
- `→` appended to every link and button.
- Numbered markers (01 / 02 / 03) on content that isn't a sequence.

## Decoration
- Glassmorphism on every surface; glow shadows on buttons and cards.
- Icon inside a tinted rounded square at the top of every feature card.
- Aurora/mesh blobs behind every section.
- Fade-and-slide-up on each section; hover lift on every card; shimmer on every button.
- Sparkle ✨ icons for anything AI.

## Copy
- Slogans instead of information: "Revolutionize your workflow", "Seamless", "Supercharge", "Unlock", "Elevate", "Next-gen", "All-in-one".
- Thai equivalents: ยกระดับ…, ปลดล็อก…, ครบจบในที่เดียว, ขับเคลื่อนด้วย AI, นวัตกรรมแห่งอนาคต.
- Generic button labels: Submit, Get Started, Learn More (when a specific verb exists).

## How to replace a tell
Ask of the subject: *what does this world actually look like?* A trading desk has order books, tick charts, dense monospaced tapes, stark red/green; a clinic has forms, calm neutrals, large legible type; a kids' toy has chunky shapes and saturated primaries. Borrow structure and texture from there, then keep everything else quiet.
